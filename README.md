## 🎯 About The Project

This is a **complete UI/UX redesign** of the IRCTC (Indian Railway Catering and Tourism Corporation) website, created as a submission for the **HE:EGU REDESIGN** college competition.

### Why IRCTC?

| | |
|---|---|
| 🇮🇳 **India's Largest Railway Network** | Millions of users daily |
| 🚂 **High User Traffic** | Poor mobile experience |
| 🎨 **Outdated Design** | High potential for improvement |
| 📱 **Mobile First Need** | Complex booking flow |

### Our Vision

> *"Booking a train ticket should be as easy as booking a cab."*

## ✨ Features

### 🏠 **Homepage**
- ✅ Fixed navigation with glassmorphism effect
- ✅ Animated hero section with gradient background
- ✅ Quick search form (From, To, Date, Passengers, Class)
- ✅ Live statistics with counter animation
- ✅ 6 service cards with hover effects
- ✅ Dark/Light mode toggle
- ✅ English/Hindi language toggle
- ✅ Mobile-responsive navigation (horizontal links on mobile)
- ✅ Footer with social links

### 🎫 **Booking Page**
- ✅ 4-step booking wizard
- ✅ Train search with filters
- ✅ Train results with price and availability
- ✅ Passenger details form
- ✅ Payment mockup
- ✅ Step progress indicator
- ✅ Confetti celebration on booking success

### 🛠️ **Services Page**
- ✅ 6 service cards with icons
- ✅ Popular/New/Latest badges
- ✅ Hover animations
- ✅ Service descriptions

### 👤 **Profile Page**
- ✅ User avatar (auto-generated)
- ✅ 3 tabs: Dashboard, Bookings, Settings
- ✅ Booking history with status badges
- ✅ Account settings form

### 🌙 **Dark Mode**
- ✅ Smooth theme toggle
- ✅ All pages support dark mode
- ✅ Professional dark theme colors
- ✅ Saves user preference

### 🇮🇳 **Bilingual Support**
- ✅ English / Hindi language toggle
- ✅ All content translated
- ✅ Saves user preference

### 📱 **Mobile Optimized**
- ✅ Fully responsive on all devices
- ✅ Navigation links visible horizontally on mobile
- ✅ Touch-friendly buttons and inputs
